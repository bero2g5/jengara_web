Ribyos is designed around one shared foundation—not separate systems for every use case. Identity, knowledge, execution and governance connect the whole product.

01 / BUILD & TEST

Studio

A workspace for developers and business workflow builders to configure agents, connect knowledge and test coordinated workflows.

  • Test runs & source references

02 / RUN & GOVERN
◈

  • Permission-aware retrieval
  • Approval queues & audit trails
  • Usage, quality & cost monitoring

Marketplace

Find reusable agents, tools, connectors and workflow packs. Begin with curated Jengara packages and controlled installation.

  • Administrator-reviewed installation

DEVELOPMENT WITH INTENT

Start focused.
Earn the next step.

We’re defining and developing Ribyos through validated increments. The first proposed slice is a document-backed research or supplier-review workflow.

  1. 01

    Connect approved knowledge.

    Create an organisation and workspace. Add a small, explicitly approved document collection.

  2. 02

    Coordinate a useful workflow.

    Configure agents, run against those documents and return a draft with verifiable references.

  3. 03

    Review, record and reuse.

    Require human review, record execution and cost, and package the workflow for reuse.

Can I use Ribyos today?+

Ribyos is in product definition and development planning. This website presents interactive concepts, not an implemented production platform. Contact Jengara Labs to discuss your workflows and product discovery.

Is Ribyos only for technology or telecom teams?+

No. Ribyos is industry-neutral. Knowledge, procurement, people, financial services and industrial workflows are part of its planned extension areas. Reliability, SRE and telecom belong in optional packs.

What does “open” mean for Ribyos?+

Model choice, interoperability and extensibility. No open-source licensing decision has been made. Application stack, model providers and deployment options are still being evaluated.

Will there be a third-party marketplace?+

The initial direction is curated Jengara-built packages with clear permissions and administrator-reviewed installation. Third-party publishing and paid marketplace transactions are later phases.

How does Ribyos relate to Jengara?+

Ribyos is a distinct product developed by Jengara Labs. Jengara is the parent brand, with Advisory and Labs practices. Advisory recommendations remain independent, with relevant product relationships disclosed.

What are the pricing and deployment options?+

Pricing, commercial packaging, licensing and deployment models have not been selected. We’ll communicate validated capabilities and release details as development progresses.
