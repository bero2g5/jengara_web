A DELIBERATE WAY TO BUILD

Small steps.
Useful evidence.

Products earn their place through validated increments. We start with a business problem and keep human judgment close to the work.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Identify a recurring problem and the decision it holds back.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Build the smallest useful path through the problem.

  3. 03

    Evaluate

    Test outputs, permissions, economics and failure modes.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Use the evidence to decide what to improve and what to release.
