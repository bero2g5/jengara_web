Jengara Labs · Product development

Building Ribyos for enterprise AI agents.

Jengara Labs is the product development practice behind Ribyos. We are building an industry-neutral platform that connects enterprise knowledge, models and tools, with human approvals and a shared governance layer. Our applied research informs the product, while Jengara Advisory continues to provide independent strategy and transformation expertise.

Ribyos · by Jengara Labs

Build, run and govern enterprise AI agents.

Ribyos is our enterprise agent platform in development. Studio helps teams build and test agents. Enterprise provides a shared workspace and governance. Marketplace extends the platform through reusable agents, connectors and industry packs.

The foundation connects knowledge, models and business systems. Industry expertise—including reliability and SRE—is an optional extension.

How Labs operates

Evidence guides product development.

Ribyos is our flagship product in development. We begin with a bounded enterprise workflow, test it against source evidence and approval requirements, then turn validated capabilities into reusable agents and packs.

Advisory × Labs

Connected insight. Protected independence.

Each engine strengthens the other without blurring its role.

What flows into Labs

Anonymized market patterns, public research questions and recurring infrastructure problems—not confidential client data by default.

What flows into Advisory

Sharper benchmarks, validated methods and a deeper view of emerging technology and operating risk.

What remains protected

Client confidentiality, vendor-neutral recommendations and explicit disclosure of any relevant commercial relationship.
