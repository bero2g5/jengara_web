Questions about Jengara Advisory

What advisory engagements does Jengara offer?

Jengara offers four focused engagements: AI Strategy & Executive Advisory, Enterprise & Agentic Transformation, AI Performance & Governance, and Private Capital & Technology Advisory. Each starts with the business decision and brings together the specialist expertise needed to address it.

Can Jengara provide executive advisory without implementation?

Yes. Executive advisory can stand alone. When implementation is needed, Jengara Labs supports applied engineering within an agreed delivery scope.

What does AI Performance & Governance cover?

The engagement focuses on measuring business results, managing cloud and AI costs, and maintaining data governance, quality, reliability and operating controls so that AI remains effective, economical and accountable.

How can an organization start working with Jengara?

Start with a confidential conversation about the decision you need to make. The advisory page identifies an executive advisory mandate, an AI Value & Readiness Assessment or a technology investment review as possible starting points. Contact hello@jengara.ai to discuss a focused engagement.
