About Jengara

AI products, grounded in experience.

Jengara is an AI software company. Jengara Labs is our product development arm, with Ribyos as our flagship enterprise AI agent platform in development. Jengara Advisory is a supporting practice providing independent AI strategy, transformation and investment expertise.

Our model

Product development leads our work.

Our mission is to turn organisational knowledge into accountable AI-driven action. Ribyos brings together Studio for building agents, Enterprise for deployment and governance, and Marketplace for reusable extensions.

Our expertise is grounded in more than 15 years of experience across global technology platforms and infrastructure.

Labs develops the shared platform. Advisory contributes domain insight while maintaining independent recommendations and clear client confidentiality boundaries.

Our structure

Labs leads. Advisory supports.

Jengara Labs develops Ribyos and the reusable knowledge, workflow and governance capabilities that support it.

Jengara Advisory delivers independent diligence, strategy and execution assurance for investors, boards and technology leaders.

Product development and advisory engagements have distinct responsibilities. We protect client confidentiality and disclose relevant commercial interests in our advice.
