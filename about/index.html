About Jengara

AI solutions, grounded in experience.

Jengara is an AI solutions company that builds products and helps organisations turn AI into practical business value. Jengara Labs is our product development arm, creating a growing portfolio of AI products around real customer needs. Jengara Advisory provides independent expertise in AI strategy, transformation and technology investment.

Our model

Product development leads our work.

Our mission is to turn organisational knowledge into accountable AI-driven action. Ribyos brings together Studio for building agents, Enterprise for deployment and governance, and Marketplace for reusable extensions.

Our expertise is grounded in more than 15 years of experience across global technology platforms and infrastructure.

Labs develops a portfolio of AI products, each with its own audience and roadmap. Advisory contributes domain insight while maintaining independent recommendations and clear client confidentiality boundaries.

Our structure

Labs leads. Advisory supports.

Jengara Labs creates AI products around real customer needs. Its growing portfolio starts with Ribyos, our enterprise agent platform in development.

Jengara Advisory delivers independent diligence, strategy and execution assurance for investors, boards and technology leaders.

Product development and advisory engagements have distinct responsibilities. We protect client confidentiality and disclose relevant commercial interests in our advice.
